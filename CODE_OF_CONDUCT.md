# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a respectful, welcoming place for everyone who uses it or
contributes to it, regardless of background, identity or experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when you disagree.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Give and accept feedback on code and ideas, never on people.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chats)
and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers at contato@id42.studio. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban participants, in proportion to the
behaviour.
